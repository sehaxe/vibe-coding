Built on OpenCode

the tool you will actually use

OpenCode is the open-source terminal agent for coding. It reads your repo, writes files, runs commands and keeps the session — the same tool you see in the demo above.

You will use it from lesson one: plan mode, build mode, tools, skills and the review loop. No toy editor, no fake sandbox — the real thing, with a human in the loop.

FAQ

the short answers
Do I need to be a programmer?

You need the basics: variables, functions, Git. The course teaches the AI workflow, not programming from zero.

How are the classes organised?

Two live 90-minute sessions a week in the evening, plus 3–4 hours of practice. Everything is recorded.

Is it online or offline?

Blended: live workshops online, recordings and async code review between sessions. The capstone demo day is online.

What if I miss a week?

Sessions are recorded and every module has a written brief. You can catch up, but the capstone needs the full six weeks.

Refunds?

Full refund up to 7 days before the start. After that, a credit for the next cohort.
