A six-week online course for IT students and junior developers. Learn to build with AI agents, review what they write, and ship a feature that survives production. Practised in OpenCode.

01

Target audience

2nd–4th year IT students and junior developers with 0–2 years of experience who already write code but use AI tools chaotically.

02

Learners’ needs

Employers expect AI literacy. Juniors need to prompt well, verify generated code, avoid security and licensing traps, and grow their speed safely.

03

Training goals

By the end, a learner can write effective prompts, embed AI in the SDLC, review and test AI code, use it securely, and measure the productivity gain.

04

Course overview

Six modules: LLM basics, prompt engineering, the agentic workflow, code review, debugging and docs, and a capstone shipped end-to-end.

05

Duration & schedule

Six weeks · two live 90-minute sessions per week · 36 hours total · evening groups · recordings and async review between sessions.

06

Admission requirements

Basic Python or JavaScript, Git fundamentals, English B1, a laptop and 3–4 hours per week. A short entry task, no exam.

07

Course format

Blended: live online workshops, recorded briefs and weekly code review. Groups of 12–16; the capstone is built in pairs.

08

Skills gained

Prompt engineering, AI code review, testing generated code, secure AI use, debugging with traces, and a portfolio-ready capstone.

09

Tuition

$290 for the full course; early bird $240 until 3 November. Includes materials, weekly code review, capstone review and the certificate.

10

Endorsement

A certificate co-signed by the IT faculty partner, with a verification link and a LinkedIn micro-credential listing the skills.
